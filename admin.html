Add MediLink to your Home Screen
On Android — Chrome
  1. Tap the menu ⋮ at the top-right of Chrome.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app).
  3. Tap Add / Install — a MediLink button appears on your home screen.
On iPhone / iPad — Safari
  1. Tap the Share button ↑ at the bottom of the screen.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add — a MediLink button appears on your home screen.
On this device
  1. Open your browser's menu.
  2. Choose Install or Add to Home screen.
  3. Confirm — a Directory button is added.
