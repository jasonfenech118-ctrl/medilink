    Add MediLink to your Home Screen
    On Android — Chrome
    1. Tap the menu ⋮ at the top-right of Chrome.
    2. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app).
    3. Tap Add / Install — a MediLink button appears on your home screen.
    On iPhone / iPad — Safari
    1. Tap the Share button ↑ at the bottom of the screen.
    2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
    3. Tap Add — a MediLink button appears on your home screen.
    On iPhone — open in Safari first
    1. iPhone only lets you add apps to the Home Screen from Safari.
    2. Copy this link, open Safari, and paste it in the address bar:
    3. In Safari, tap Share ↑ → Add to Home Screen → Add.
    Inside WhatsApp, Gmail or Messenger? Use their menu's Open in Safari / Open in browser option. In Safari, avoid a Private tab — it hides Add to Home Screen.
    On this device
